Access
By Public Transport
(JR Line) 10 minutes’ walk from Ueno or Uguisudani Station
(Ginza or Hibiya Tokyo Metro Line) 15 minutes' walk from Ueno Station
(Chiyoda Tokyo Metro Line) 15 minutes' walk from Nezu Station
(Keisei Line) 15 minutes' walk from Keisei Ueno Station
Visitor entrance is via the Main gate.
Enlarge
By Car
5 mins from Ueno Exit, off the Shuto Expressway Ueno Route.
For visitors with wheelchairs, please consult our General Affairs section.
Parking facilities are available near Ueno station, as listed below.
Ueno Park Parking No. 1 (for buses) (Ueno Station Park Exit)
Tel. 03-3821-0755
Ueno Park Parking No. 2 (for buses) (Ueno Station Park Exit)
Tel. 03-3821-0755

There is no parking at the museum.
Hours
9:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
Open 9:30 a.m. – 8:00 p.m. on Friday, Saturday, and on Sundays when the following Monday is a holiday.
* Last admission 30 minutes before closing.
* May 4 and May 5 : Open until 8:00 p.m.
* January 1, 2027 : Open from 12:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
* Opening hours for special exhibitions may differ.
* Opening hours for the Research and Information Center  may differ.
* Kuroda Memorial Hall is open until 5:00 p.m.
Admissions ＆ Tickets
To ensure the safety and security of our visitors and the Museum, security screening using metal detectors and baggage inspections has been in place at the Main Gate since Tuesday, September 8
To ensure the safety and security of the museum and our visitors, bringing suitcases into the museum is not allowed from August 1 (Sat) onwards.
We seek your understanding and assistance in this matter as we strive to create a safe environment for our visitors.
Waiting times at the main gate ticket counters may exceed 30 minutes during busy periods. We recommend purchasing tickets online for smoother entry into the museum.
Special Exhibitions
For special exhibition ticketing, please see the respective official exhibition websites. Tickets for special exhibitions can also be used to visit the TNM Collection Exhibitions on the same day.

Purchasing TNM Collection Exhibitions tickets
TNM Collection Exhibitions tickets can be purchased on your smartphones and other devices. The tickets will be valid (any time during the Museum's opening hours) for two months from the date of purchase.
Note: Visitors who wish to enter only the Kuroda Memorial Hall and/or Research and Information Center are admitted free of charge.

Visitors with special exhibition tickets

High/Junior High/Elementary School Students and persons under 18 and over 70
Free admission. Please present proof of age when entering the museum.
Persons with disabilities and one caregiver of individuals with disabilities
Free admission. Please present a certificate of disabilities when entering the museum.
Payment options
Payments by the following are accepted at ticket counters:
Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, JCB, AMEX, Diners, DISCOVER)
e-cash (iD, Suica,WAON)
UnionPay
Free admission days (Apply only to the TNM Collection Exhibitions)
Free admission to the TNM Collection Exhibitions on May 19, September 21, and November 3, 2026.
Closed
Mondays (Excluding holidays. On those days, the Museum will be open on Monday and closed the following day)
Year-end closure: From December 26 to December 31, 2026.
* The museum will be open from January 1, 2027 (Friday, national holiday), from 12:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
* Subject to change.
Exceptions for 2026:
April 7, 2026: The museum will be temporarily closed for maintenance of the exhibition environment.
The museum will be open on April 27, 2026; August 10, 2026; September 21, 2026; and March 29, 2027.
December 15, 2026: The museum will be closed for facility inspection and maintenance.
At the end of 2026, the museum will be open until December 25.
However, from December 22 to December 25, only the Horyu-ji Treasures Gallery and the Kuroda Memorial Hall will be open.
The museum will be open from January 1, 2027 (Friday, national holiday), from 12:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Temporary partial closures:
